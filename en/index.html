        Company packages

        Subscribe monthly or yearly and get a fixed number of government services every month for less than single prices. Nukhba and Gold are for larger companies and cover all government services.

        Package terms, clearly

        What counts?
        Each transaction for one person or one company counts separately: renewing 5 employees' residence permits counts as 5. A transaction counts when it is submitted to the authority.
        How many services does a transaction use?
        By its single price: up to 400 QAR uses one, 401 to 750 QAR uses two, above 750 QAR uses three.
        Which services are covered?
        Asas and Shamel: only the core services marked "In packages" (51 services). Nukhba and Gold: all government services (90 services).
        What is not covered?
        Government fees, setup bundles (except one free setup bundle a year in Gold), studies and consulting, and non-core services in Asas and Shamel. These are paid separately with your package discount.
        If you exceed your package?
        Extra transactions are charged at the single price minus your package discount: 15% Asas, 25% Shamel, 30% Nukhba, 35% Gold.
        If you do not use them all?
        The balance rolls over to the next month only, then expires.
        Term and payment
        Monthly subscriptions run for at least 3 months, paid in advance each month. Yearly subscriptions are paid in advance and cost 10 months.

        Frequently asked questions

        Does the price include government fees?

        No. The price is Basira's fee only. Government fees are paid exactly as on the official receipt, which we hand to you.

        Do you guarantee approval or timing?

        Nobody can guarantee a government authority's decision or timing. We guarantee that we check the documents before submission and keep you informed of every update.

        What if I do not use all package services in a month?

        Unused services roll over to the next month only.

        How are studies and consulting priced?

        The published price is the starting price. After a short discovery session we send a written quotation with scope, deliverables and timing. 50% is paid at the start and 50% on delivery.

        Do you offer legal or accounting advice?

        Yes, together with licensed law and accounting firms. We coordinate with them and follow up on your behalf. Statutory audits are performed by a licensed auditor.

        Which documents do I need?

        The required documents are listed in each service's details; click the service name to see them. After reviewing your request we confirm the final list before we start.

        What if the transaction needs additional documents?

        We tell you exactly what the authority asks for and complete the submission once it is provided. If this changes the cost, we tell you before we continue.

        How do packages work, and what do they not cover?

        You get a fixed number of government services each month. Asas and Shamel cover the core services only; Nukhba and Gold cover all government services. Setup bundles, studies and consulting are outside the count in every package. See the package terms.

        How do you handle my documents and personal data?

        We use them only to complete your transaction, and we ask for documents only through our official channel after reviewing your request. See the privacy policy.

        How do I pay?

        Single services are paid on agreement, setup bundles and studies 50% at the start and 50% on delivery, and subscriptions in advance.

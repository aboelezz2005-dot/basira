      Frequently asked questions

      Does the price include government fees?

      No. The price is Basira's fee only. Government fees are paid exactly as on the official receipt, which we hand to you.

      Do you guarantee approval or timing?

      Nobody can guarantee a government authority's decision or timing. We guarantee that we check the documents before submission and keep you informed of every update.

      What if I do not use all package services in a month?

      Unused services roll over to the next month only.

      How are studies and consulting priced?

      The published price is the starting price. After a short discovery session we send a written quotation with scope, deliverables and timing. 50% is paid at the start and 50% on delivery.

      Do you offer legal or accounting advice?

      Yes, together with licensed law and accounting firms. We coordinate with them and follow up on your behalf. Statutory audits are performed by a licensed auditor.

      How do I pay?

      Single services are paid on agreement, setup bundles and studies 50% at the start and 50% on delivery, and subscriptions in advance.
